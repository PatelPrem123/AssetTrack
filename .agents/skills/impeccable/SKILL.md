# Impeccable Design Audit Skill

Based on [`github.com/pbakaus/impeccable`](https://github.com/pbakaus/impeccable).

## Focus Areas

1. **Aesthetic Audit:** Inspect alignment, padding balance, and typography contrast in Tailwind CSS.
2. **Chart Tuning:** Enhance Recharts graphs (category distributions, status breakdowns).
3. **Print Layouts:** Verify exact sizing for printable QR asset labels (2x1 and 3x2 inch layouts).
